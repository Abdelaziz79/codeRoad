# Welcome to `Dev Learn`

###### `Dev Learn` is your ultimate destination for learning and mastering coding skills. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced programmer, we provide a platform where you can explore various topics, take quizzes to test your knowledge, engage with the community through posts and comments, and continuously improve your coding abilities.

## What We Offer

#### 1. **Markdown Topics Creation**

- Create and explore topics using Markdown, allowing for easy formatting and organization. Markdown makes it simple to structure your content and share it with others.![Screenshot 2024-04-17 181914](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0380fb01-7a49-4868-a238-6339ce196cb4)

#### 2. **Quiz Creation and Results Storage**

- Challenge yourself with quizzes on a wide range of coding topics. Track your progress and see how your skills improve over time. Your quiz results are securely stored, allowing you to review them whenever you need.![Screenshot 2024-04-17 183738](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ba12b16e-a768-4f75-8a8f-38e215b96248)

#### 3. **Post Creation, Update, and Deletion**

- Share your insights, experiences, and questions with the community through posts. Update or delete your posts as needed to keep the conversation relevant and engaging.![Screenshot 2024-04-18 183330](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4ac1a8a0-194f-41fe-844d-b20af14630b8)

#### 4. **Like, Dislike, and Comment Features**

- Interact with posts by liking, disliking, and commenting. Engage in discussions, offer feedback, and connect with fellow learners and experts in the field.![Screenshot 2024-04-18 191924](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6b4334c1-b3e2-4d12-b786-f772e22fa9d8)

#### 5. **User Account Management**

- Create a user account to personalize your experience on `Dev Learn`. Easily update your name and password to keep your account secure and up to date.![Screenshot 2024-04-18 182658](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b7b2edcf-5a2c-4ea2-82a1-ef21fa7b8721)

#### 6. **Comprehensive Search Functionality**

- Effortlessly find the topics you're interested in by searching based on level, title, or topic name. Our advanced search feature helps you quickly locate the information you need to continue your learning journey.
  ![Screenshot 2024-04-18 191024](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1004c152-0257-4c07-8317-9a47de20efe2)

#### 7. **Admin Page**

- Manage users, topics, and reported posts with ease through our dedicated admin page. Ensure the community remains a positive and productive environment for all users.![Screenshot 2024-04-17 183632](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8fa11f50-03c6-4baa-8e93-14a177a64411)

#### 8. **Interactive Code Execution**

- Utilize LiveCodes to run code in C++, JavaScript, and TypeScript directly within the platform. Practice coding in real-time and see immediate results.![photo_2024-07-19_11-39-49](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d30e5108-98aa-43f2-8492-3e68582f9b88)

#### 9. **Responsive UI Components**

- Enhance your user interface with React Bootstrap, ensuring your application is both responsive and visually appealing.

#### 10. **Client-Side Routing**

- Navigate seamlessly between different sections of the application using React Router, providing a smooth and dynamic user experience.

### **Get Started Today**

- Embark on your coding journey with `Dev Learn`. Whether you're looking to learn the basics or dive deep into advanced concepts, we're here to support you every step of the way. Join our community of passionate learners and take your coding skills to new heights.

## Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, React Query, Context API, React Bootstrap, React Router, Recharts
- **Backend:** Supabase (for Supabase backend version), .NET (for .NET backend version)
- **Code Execution:** LiveCodes (C++, JavaScript, TypeScript)
- **HTTP Client:** Axios
- **Deployment:** Vercel
